# I fixed my own dishwasher and I have opinions

It stopped draining on a Tuesday. Standing water, maybe two inches, smelling
like the inside of a gym bag. The internet said: check the filter. The filter
was fine. The internet then said: it is the drain pump, that will be $340
installed.

It was not the drain pump.

Here is the thing nobody puts in the first three search results. There is a
small rubber flap where the drain hose meets the disposal, and if you installed
a new disposal in the last few years, there is a decent chance whoever did it
left the knockout plug in. Mine had been in there since 2019. The dishwasher
had been draining through a hole the size of a pencil eraser for six years and
finally gave up.

Getting it out took four minutes and a screwdriver. I want to be clear that I
am not handy. I have broken two things trying to fix one thing on more than one
occasion. This was genuinely four minutes.

What annoys me is not that I didn't know. It is that three separate repair
guides walked me through disassembling the pump housing, which is a forty
minute job with a real chance of breaking a clip, before anything mentioned the
knockout plug. The cheap check should come first. It almost never does, and I
suspect that is because the people writing repair guides are the people who
sell parts.

Anyway. Two inches of gym bag water, four minutes, zero dollars. Check the
knockout plug.
