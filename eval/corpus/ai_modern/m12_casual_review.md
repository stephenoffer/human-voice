# Two months with an e-bike commute

I bought an e-bike to replace a twenty-minute car commute, and the thing nobody mentioned in the reviews is how much the weather stops mattering. Not because the bike handles rain well, but because you are moving fast enough that a shower is over before it becomes a problem.

The commute takes about the same time as driving, which surprised me. I expected to lose time and gain exercise. What actually happens is that I skip the parking search at both ends, so the door-to-door number comes out even while the moving time is longer.

The practical downsides are real. Charging is fine at home and awkward anywhere else, the bike is heavy enough that a flat tire is a genuine inconvenience rather than a minor one, and I have become the sort of person who thinks about bike theft. I bought a lock that cost more than I wanted to spend and I still bring the battery inside.

Would I recommend it? For a commute under about eight miles with somewhere secure to park, yes, without much hesitation. Beyond that the calculation gets harder.
