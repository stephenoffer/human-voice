# Introducing scheduled reports

Getting the right data to the right people has always required someone to
remember to send it. Scheduled reports change that, allowing you to define a
report once and have it delivered automatically on the cadence your team
actually works in.

## How it works

You build a report the way you already do, using the filters and columns that
matter to your workflow. From there, you choose a schedule, add recipients, and
the report arrives in their inbox as a PDF or a CSV attachment. Recipients do
not need an account, meaning you can send a weekly summary to a client or an
executive without provisioning a seat for them.

## Why teams are using it

The most common use is the Monday morning digest. Instead of one person pulling
numbers and pasting them into an email, the report goes out automatically at
8:00 a.m. in each recipient's local timezone, freeing that person to spend the
first hour of the week on something other than copy and paste.

Teams also use scheduled reports for compliance. If your auditor requires a
monthly access log, you can schedule it to their address directly, creating a
paper trail without a recurring task on anyone's calendar.
