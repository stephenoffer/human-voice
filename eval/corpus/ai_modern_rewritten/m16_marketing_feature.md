# Introducing scheduled reports

Somebody on your team remembers to send the numbers every Monday. Scheduled
reports take that job off them. You build the report once, pick a cadence, and
it goes out on its own.

Build it the way you already do, with whatever filters and columns you use.
Then choose a schedule and add recipients. The report lands in their inbox as a
PDF or a CSV. Recipients do not need an account, so you can send a weekly
summary to a client or a board member without buying them a seat.

The Monday digest is what most teams set up first. It goes out at 8:00 a.m. in
each recipient's local timezone, and whoever used to assemble it gets their
first hour back.

Compliance is the other common one. If your auditor wants a monthly access log,
send it straight to their address. No recurring task on anyone's calendar, and
a paper trail that exists whether or not someone remembered.

Scheduled reports are on Growth and Enterprise now, at no extra cost. Existing
customers will find the option in the report toolbar today. If you are on
Starter and this is the thing you need, upgrading takes about a minute and the
price has not changed.
