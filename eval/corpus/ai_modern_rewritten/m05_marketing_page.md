# Ship your changelog without writing it

Your release notes already exist. They're scattered across your merge commits and
the Slack thread where somebody finally explained what changed.

Tag a release and Notchup drafts the entry. You edit the parts that matter and
publish. Friday afternoon archaeology becomes a five-minute review.

The time saved isn't what teams tell us about, though. It's that the changelog
actually goes out now. Writing it used to be the chore that landed on whoever had
the lightest week. So it slipped. Then it slipped again, and customers stopped
checking.

Works with GitHub and GitLab, self-hosted included. Publishes to a hosted page or
your own site. Public repos are free; pay when you need private ones.
