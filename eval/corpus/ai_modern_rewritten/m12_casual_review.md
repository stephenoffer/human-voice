# Two months with an e-bike commute

Nobody in the reviews mentioned the thing that actually changed: weather stopped
mattering. Not because the bike handles rain well. Because you're moving fast enough
that a shower ends before it becomes your problem.

It also takes about as long as driving, which I didn't expect. I'd assumed I'd trade
time for exercise. What happens instead is that I skip the parking hunt at both
ends, so door to door comes out even while the moving part takes longer.

The downsides are real and dull. Charging is easy at home and awkward anywhere else.
The bike is heavy enough that a flat tire ruins an afternoon rather than fifteen
minutes. And I've become a person who thinks about bike theft, owns a lock that cost
more than I wanted to spend, and still carries the battery inside every night.

Under about eight miles, with somewhere secure to park it, yes. Past that the math
gets harder and I'd think about it.
