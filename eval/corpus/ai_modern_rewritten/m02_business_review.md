# Q3 retention review

Retention went 71% to 76%. Onboarding did it. The July cohort retained at 81%; the
week before the change, 68%. Eight weeks on, the gap hasn't closed, so this isn't
people being briefly charmed by a new screen.

Mid-market didn't move. Those accounts churn at about twice the enterprise rate and
the exit interviews all say the same thing, which is that they can't get their data
in without borrowing an engineer from us. We learned that in March. We did nothing
about it.

Spend Q4 on the import path. Mid-market is 40% of new logos and 55% of churn, so
fixing it compounds; another dashboard does not. Product's objection is that import
work is unglamorous and impossible to demo, and they're right on both counts, and
the arithmetic doesn't care.

The risk is the estimate. Six weeks, from a team whose estimates in this area have
run long every time. Miss the January renewal cluster and Q1 absorbs it quietly.
