# Proposal: consolidate the three support queues

Do it, after the busy season.

Three products, three ticketing systems, which made sense when three different
buyers purchased them. About a third of our accounts now use two or more, and their
requests bounce between queues while the clock runs.

Consolidating means moving everything onto the platform the largest team already
lives in, with product as a routing tag instead of a separate instance. Licensing
savings are real and small, roughly eighteen thousand a year. The part worth having
is that one ticket can cover two products without a handoff, and handoffs are where
our worst resolution times come from.

Migration is the cost. Two teams built reporting on their current instance and that
work has to be redone, which we put at three weeks of one engineer. Training costs
more than people expect, because agents have muscle memory for their tools and you
are about to break it.

Savings alone wouldn't justify the disruption. The handoff problem would, and it
grows every quarter the account mix shifts, which is the argument for doing it now
rather than discovering the price later.
